CyberSentry AI

CyberSentry AI is a cutting‑edge cyber security platform developed to identify, mitigate and prevent digital threats using the power of artificial intelligence. As cyber risks evolve, traditional defences are no longer sufficient. CyberSentry blends machine learning, behavioural analytics and real‑time automation to deliver comprehensive protection across networks, endpoints and cloud services.

Features of the platform include:

  • Smart Detection: AI models trained to recognise anomalies and attack patterns in massive data streams.
  • User Behaviour Analytics: Continuous monitoring of user activities to detect insider threats and compromised accounts.
  • Automated Response: Real‑time isolation of threats and dynamic policy adjustments without manual intervention.
  • Scalable Architecture: Deployable on‑premises or in the cloud with multi‑tenant support.

As Technical Director, Arsalan leads the development and strategic vision of CyberSentry AI, ensuring it remains at the forefront of cyber defence technology.

Data Mobility

Data Mobility was created to redefine the way we access and secure information. By combining advanced virtualisation with robust encryption, Data Mobility enables users to work anywhere without compromising performance or privacy. The platform facilitates seamless data access across devices and networks, empowering businesses to embrace remote work and global collaboration.

Key highlights include:

  • Secure Virtualisation: Isolated environments for applications and data, protecting them from malware and unauthorised access.
  • End‑to‑End Encryption: All data is encrypted in transit and at rest, ensuring confidentiality at every stage.
  • Flexible Deployment: Options for private cloud, hybrid setups or on‑premise systems tailored to organisational needs.
  • Ease of Use: Designed with user experience in mind, the platform integrates seamlessly into existing workflows.

Under Arsalan’s leadership, Data Mobility continues to evolve, helping organisations stay agile and secure in an increasingly interconnected world.
