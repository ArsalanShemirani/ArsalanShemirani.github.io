CyberSentry AI

CyberSentry AI is a next-generation cyber defence platform that leverages machine learning to detect threats in real time. Here we share the technical underpinnings, including our use of unsupervised anomaly detection, neural network architectures and adaptive response algorithms.

Model Architecture

The core of CyberSentry AI is an ensemble of deep neural networks trained on millions of network behaviour patterns. These networks learn the normal behaviour of your systems and detect deviations that may indicate malicious activity.

Data Engineering

We use a scalable data pipeline built on streaming frameworks to ingest, normalise and feature-engineer terabytes of event data per day. Our feature extraction process focuses on capturing temporal dynamics and contextual relationships across events.

Data Mobility

Data Mobility provides secure, flexible and high-performance data management across diverse environments. This section explains our virtualisation architecture, encryption algorithms and the mobile access protocols that enable seamless connectivity while preserving confidentiality.

Virtualisation Engine

At the heart of Data Mobility lies a containerised virtualisation engine that isolates user sessions and data streams. This engine uses kernel-level isolation to ensure that data access remains secure even on untrusted devices.

Encryption & Key Management

We implement end-to-end encryption using AES-256 for data at rest and TLS 1.3 for data in transit, combined with hardware-backed key storage and multi-factor authentication.
