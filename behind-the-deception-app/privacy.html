Privacy policy

Your channel data stays under your control.

This policy describes the private, owner-operated Behind The Deception channel workflow. It is not a service for collecting other creators' channel data.

Data accessed

With the owner's authorization, the application accesses the connected channel's identity, video metadata and available analytics, such as views, watch time, viewing duration, retention, traffic sources and subscriber changes. Availability depends on YouTube's reports and the permissions granted.

For optional private uploads, the application reads the owner's selected local video file and upload metadata and sends them to YouTube. It stores the resulting video identifier and upload status to avoid duplicate submissions.

Permissions requested

  • youtube.readonly: identify the authorized channel and read channel/video information.
  • yt-analytics.readonly: retrieve authorized channel analytics reports.
  • youtube.upload: requested only for the optional upload profile, to upload selected approved videos. The workflow sets them to Private.

The application does not request Gmail or Google Drive access. Google authorization occurs on Google's own pages; the application does not request the owner's Google password.

How data is used

Channel data is used to maintain a private performance dashboard, compare videos, diagnose production or reporting faults and prepare editorial recommendations. Upload records support the requested private-draft upload workflow.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials are stored in private local files with restricted filesystem permissions. They are not intended to be included in prompts, public pages or shared project files. Analytics snapshots, summaries, generation records and workflow logs are stored locally in the owner's project.

There is currently no automatic deletion schedule for analytics snapshots and workflow records. They are retained under the owner's control until deleted or an implemented retention policy changes this behavior. Revoking Google access stops future authorized collection; it does not automatically erase local copies or information already submitted to other services.

Service providers and AI-assisted reports

Google processes authorization, analytics requests and any requested uploads. Limited analytics summaries and channel information may be submitted to the owner's Claude or Codex services to generate private reports and production recommendations. These providers process submitted content under the owner's account settings and their applicable terms. OAuth tokens, client secrets and recovery information must not be included in these submissions.

The application does not sell channel data, supply it to data brokers or use it for advertising, retargeting, credit or lending decisions. Any transfer to an AI service must be limited to the owner-authorized reporting feature and permitted by the applicable Google policies and provider settings; the workflow must not use API data to train a general-purpose model.

Google API data commitments

The application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including applicable Limited Use requirements. This statement is a data-use commitment, not a claim that Google has verified or certified the application.

Revoke access or delete local data

The owner can revoke the application's access from Google Account connections. The owner can then delete local OAuth credential files, analytics snapshots, generated summaries and upload records using the project setup instructions. Copies in backups or an AI-provider conversation must be managed separately through the relevant storage or provider controls.

Visitors and hosting

These static pages contain no analytics scripts, advertising, external fonts or sign-in form. These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages at arsalanshemirani.github.io. GitHub may process standard request information, such as IP address, requested URL and browser information, under its own privacy practices.

Operator and questions

The application is operated privately by the owner of Behind The Deception. It is not open to third-party users. For questions, contact the operator through the operator contact page. This app is for the operator’s own channel; it does not offer account access to the public.

Changes

The owner should update this policy when actual permissions, providers, hosting or retention behavior changes. Material new data uses require the appropriate notice and authorization before being enabled.
