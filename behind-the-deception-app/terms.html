Application terms

A private tool for one channel.

These terms describe the intended operation of the Behind The Deception production and analytics workflow. The tool is operated by the channel owner and is not offered as a public service.

Authorized operation

Use is limited to the owner and people the owner explicitly authorizes. Connect only an account and channel you are entitled to manage. Complete Google authentication and any identity checks yourself; do not share passwords or verification codes with an AI assistant.

What the workflow does

The workflow collects permitted analytics, prepares private production materials and may upload approved local videos as Private using a separately authorized upload profile. Public publishing, public scheduling, comments and external promotion remain subject to the owner's separate approval.

Editorial and release responsibility

The owner is responsible for reviewing factual claims, source attribution, asset permissions, disclosures, privacy and the final video before release. Automated checks and AI-generated recommendations are aids to review; they are not proof of accuracy, permission, policy approval or legal compliance.

Limits and availability

YouTube reports may be delayed, incomplete or revised. Third-party services, quotas, authorizations and local-machine availability can interrupt operation. The workflow does not guarantee uninterrupted collection, audience growth, monetization, virality or revenue.

Costs and external services

Paid generation is permitted only through the owner's configured budget controls and approvals. Google, YouTube, Claude, Codex and any approved generation service have their own applicable terms. This tool does not override those terms or grant rights to third-party material.

Data and access removal

See the privacy policy for scopes, local storage, AI-assisted reports, retention and revocation. The owner can stop the workflow and revoke Google authorization at any time.

Application status

This is an internal application under development. These informational pages do not establish that Google has verified the application.

Operator

The operator is the owner of Behind The Deception. Information and contact options are available through the operator website. The application is not currently available for third-party account connections.
