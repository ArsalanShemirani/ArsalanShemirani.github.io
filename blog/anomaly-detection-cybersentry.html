Anomaly Detection in CyberSentry AI

How we use machine learning to anticipate and neutralize threats.

Posted on September 27, 2025

Overview

CyberSentry AI relies on a combination of unsupervised learning and statistical analysis to detect anomalies in network traffic and user behaviour. Unlike signature-based approaches that look for known threats, our platform builds a model of normal behaviour and flags deviations that may indicate an intrusion or misuse.

Model Selection

At the heart of our anomaly detection pipeline are autoencoders and clustering algorithms. Autoencoders compress high-dimensional data into a latent representation and then reconstruct it, measuring the reconstruction error as an indicator of abnormality. We also use clustering techniques such as DBSCAN and Isolation Forest to identify outliers in feature space.

Training Data

Training these models requires a diverse and representative dataset. We gather clean baseline data from production environments, simulate attack scenarios and augment with synthetic anomalies. Pre-processing includes normalisation, time-window segmentation and feature scaling to ensure that models generalise well across different systems.

Results & Next Steps

Preliminary results show that our hybrid approach significantly reduces false positives while maintaining high detection rates. Future work will explore online learning to adapt to evolving behaviour patterns and integrate feedback loops from security analysts.
