About Arsalan

Discover the story behind the entrepreneur and innovator.

Background

Arsalan Shemirani is a technology entrepreneur and thought leader with a passion for crafting secure and intelligent digital systems. He began his career in software engineering, honing his skills in network infrastructure and cyber security while working with diverse teams across the globe. This experience inspired him to combine innovation and practical problem solving in every project he takes on.

Driven by curiosity and a desire to make technology accessible, Arsalan has launched multiple ventures that push the boundaries of what is possible. His work spans artificial intelligence, data encryption and seamless mobility solutions – all designed to make technology work better for people and organisations.

Entrepreneurial Journey

In 2019, Arsalan founded Data Mobility, a company focused on delivering secure and flexible solutions for remote connectivity and data management. Through innovative virtualisation and encryption technology, Data Mobility empowers businesses to access information safely from anywhere, without compromising performance or privacy.

Building on this success, he established CyberSentry AI – a next‑generation cyber security platform that harnesses artificial intelligence to detect and respond to threats in real time. As Technical Director, Arsalan leads the vision and engineering team, ensuring the platform remains at the cutting edge of AI‑driven security.

Values & Vision

Arsalan’s work is guided by a core set of values: integrity, innovation, and inclusion. He believes that technology should serve people and communities, not the other way around. His vision is to create products that not only solve today’s problems but also anticipate the challenges of tomorrow – always with ethics and responsibility at the forefront.
