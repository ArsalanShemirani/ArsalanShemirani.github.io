Private owner-operated application

Evidence-led videos.
A measured production workflow.

Behind The Deception uses a local workflow to prepare documentary videos, review channel performance and upload approved drafts privately to its own YouTube channel.

Understand performance

Read authorized channel metadata and analytics to produce private performance summaries and editorial recommendations.

Prepare a review copy

An optional, separately authorized upload profile sends approved video files to the owner's channel as Private. It does not automatically publish them.

Keep an owner in control

The owner reviews scripts, assets and the finished video. Public release remains a separate decision.

Who can use this application?

This is an internal tool for the channel owner, not a public account-registration or analytics service. This informational site does not sign visitors in or collect Google credentials.

Google authorization

Authorization takes place through Google's own consent screens and a local desktop callback. Read-only analytics and optional uploading have separate access profiles. Read the privacy policy for data handling and access removal.

This is an owner-operated application. This informational website does not indicate Google verification or approval.
